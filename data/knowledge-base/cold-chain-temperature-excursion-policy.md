# Caldova Cold-Chain Temperature Excursion Policy

**Owner:** Caldova Global Quality & Distribution
**Applies to:** All temperature-controlled (cold-chain) products distributed by Caldova Pharmaceuticals.

## 1. Cold-chain range

Caldova cold-chain products (biologics and vaccines such as **Allerveo**, **Caldovir**, and **Immunova**) must be stored and transported at **2-8 °C** at all times. Ambient products (e.g., **Neurindra**) are handled at 15-25 °C and are out of scope for this policy.

## 2. What counts as an excursion

A **temperature excursion** is any recorded temperature outside the approved range for the product, at any point in the chain of custody (handoff scans, in-transit loggers, or last-mile delivery).

| Excursion severity | Definition | Default disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | ≤ 2 °C outside range for ≤ 1 hour, single event | Quality review; often still usable per stability data |
| **Major** | 2-8 °C above the upper limit, or > 1 hour cumulative | **Quarantine** the shipment; product is presumed non-usable pending review |
| **Critical** | > 8 °C above the upper limit, or freezing of a 2-8 °C product | **Reject**; product is non-usable and must be replaced |

## 3. Eligibility for replacement or credit

- A shipment with a **Major** or **Critical** excursion is **eligible for replacement** at no cost to the site, regardless of delivery date.
- A **Minor** excursion is **conditionally eligible** — replacement only if stability review determines the product is out of specification.
- Excursions caused by the **site** after successful in-range delivery (e.g., the site's own fridge failure) are **not** Caldova's responsibility.

## 4. Required actions on an excursion

1. Look up the shipment and its full chain of custody (handoff temperatures).
2. Classify the excursion severity from the peak temperature and duration.
3. Quarantine (Major) or reject (Critical) the affected product.
4. If eligible, issue a replacement per the [Returns, Replacement & Credit Policy](returns-replacement-and-credit-policy.md).
5. Open a CAPA (corrective and preventive action) referencing the responsible leg (carrier + distribution center).

## 5. Cold-chain root causes to check

- Reefer (refrigerated) unit fault at a distribution center or in transit.
- Dry-ice or gel-pack shortage / insufficient re-icing at a handoff.
- Extended dwell time on a loading dock during a heat event.
- Last-mile ambient exposure.

> For a shipment showing an **excursion** temperature_status, always confirm the peak temperature and the responsible handoff before deciding disposition.
