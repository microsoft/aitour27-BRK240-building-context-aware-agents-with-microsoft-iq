# Caldova Returns, Replacement & Credit Policy

**Owner:** Caldova Customer Operations & Quality
**Applies to:** Site (hospital, pharmacy, distributor) requests for return, replacement, or credit on Caldova shipments.

## 1. Eligibility rules

- **Temperature excursion (cold chain):** Products with a **Major** or **Critical** excursion (see the [Cold-Chain Temperature Excursion Policy](cold-chain-temperature-excursion-policy.md)) are **eligible for free replacement**, regardless of delivery date.
- **Damaged in transit:** Physically damaged product (broken vials, crushed packaging) is eligible for replacement when reported within **7 days** of delivery with photo evidence.
- **Short or wrong shipment:** Quantity or product discrepancies are eligible for correction/replacement within **14 days** of delivery.
- **Delayed shipment:** A shipment that misses its SLA delivery window may qualify for a **credit** (see the [Good Distribution Practice SLA](good-distribution-practice-sla.md)); the product itself is only replaced if it also fails a quality check on arrival.
- **Expired / near-expiry:** Product delivered with less than the agreed remaining shelf life is eligible for replacement.

## 2. Approval thresholds

| Replacement / credit value | Approval level |
|---|---|
| ≤ $5,000 | Auto-approve |
| $5,000 - $25,000 | Distribution manager approval |
| > $25,000 | VP of Supply Chain approval |

## 3. Disposition options

1. **Replacement** — reship equivalent product from the nearest cold-chain-capable distribution center.
2. **Credit** — apply the order value (or a partial SLA credit) to the site's account.
3. **Return** — arrange return only when required for a recall or quality investigation; most excursion/damage cases are **destroy-in-place** with evidence, no physical return.

## 4. Policy exceptions

- A prior written commitment from Caldova (e.g., "we will expedite a replacement") overrides standard timeframes.
- Long-standing sites (account since 3+ years) may receive goodwill replacement at distribution-manager discretion.
- Recalled lots are always replaced or credited regardless of timeframe.

## 5. Required steps before deciding

- Always look up the shipment, its status, and its chain of custody in the supply data before replying.
- For any cold-chain product, confirm the temperature_status and classify any excursion first.
- Cite the applicable policy section and the approval tier in the response.
