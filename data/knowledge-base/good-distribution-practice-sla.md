# Caldova Good Distribution Practice (GDP) Service Levels

**Owner:** Caldova Global Distribution
**Applies to:** All Caldova shipments and the distribution-center / carrier network.

## 1. Delivery service levels (OTIF)

Caldova measures **on-time in-full (OTIF)** delivery against the estimated arrival for each shipment.

| Product class | Standard delivery window | Cold-chain requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Cold-chain biologic / vaccine | 2-4 business days | Maintain 2-8 °C end to end |
| Ambient small molecule | 3-5 business days | 15-25 °C |

- A shipment delivered after its estimated arrival is **late** and may qualify the site for an SLA credit (see [Returns, Replacement & Credit Policy](returns-replacement-and-credit-policy.md)).
- "Lost" is declared if there is no chain-of-custody scan for **5 consecutive business days**, or no delivery within **14 business days** of ship date.

## 2. Chain of custody

Every shipment is scanned at each distribution-center **handoff**, capturing arrival time, departure time, and temperature. The chain of custody is the authoritative record for investigating delays and excursions.

- **Dwell time** at a distribution center = departed_at − arrived_at.
- **Transit time** between centers = next arrived_at − current departed_at.
- Excessive dwell during a heat event is a common cold-chain risk and must be flagged.

## 3. Distribution-center responsibilities

- Cold-chain-capable centers (see the distribution-center master) must maintain reefer capacity and re-ice shipments at each handoff.
- Carriers must be **cold-chain certified** to move biologics/vaccines; non-certified carriers may only move ambient product.

## 4. Escalation triggers

Escalate to the supply assurance team when:

- A cold-chain shipment records an **excursion** at any handoff.
- A shipment is **delayed** beyond its SLA window.
- A site reports a delivery problem (damage, short shipment, warm arrival).

## 5. External-factor checks

When a delay or risk may be caused by outside events (weather, carrier disruption, recalls), verify with real-time web sources before concluding. Internal supply data does not contain real-world events.
