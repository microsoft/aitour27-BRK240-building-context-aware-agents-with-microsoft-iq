## Steps

1. **Teams** — hover the **Caldova** agent. Point out it's an **Agent 365 autopilot**
   with its **own blueprint identity** that **reports to you**.
2. Ask it the Work IQ question — now it reads from **its own** mailbox:
   > Any urgent supply escalations in your mailbox?

   It finds **Maria Garcia's** SHP-1234 (Alvexa) escalation.
3. Walk the other three IQs, one at a time:
   - **Foundry IQ:** *Is a major cold-chain excursion eligible for replacement and credit?*
   - **Web IQ:** *Any weather or carrier disruptions this week that could affect the route?*
   - **Fabric IQ:** *Rank suppliers by latest OTIF and flag any with open regulatory actions.*
4. Ask it to handle the escalation end-to-end and **reply to Maria**.
5. **Outlook (agent mailbox)** → show the **sent reply** — proof the autopilot acted.
6. **Admin center** → show the agent **template** and the **instance**; note it needs a
   Microsoft Agent 365 license, and that it **reports to you** in the org.
7. **Teams Developer Portal** → show the **messaging endpoint** that wires the
   autopilot's hosted container to Teams.
